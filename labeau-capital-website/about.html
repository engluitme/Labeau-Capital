About Labeau Capital

A focused asset-holding company committed to responsible ownership and long-term value creation in Zimbabwe.

Who We Are

Parent Company Focused on Assets

Labeau Capital operates as an asset-holding company. Our primary role is to own and manage productive assets that support the commercial activities of our associated operating businesses.

We do not directly deliver customer-facing services. Instead, we provide the asset foundation that enables LBM Trucks and Palatial Construction to serve their clients effectively.

This structure allows clear accountability: Labeau Capital concentrates on ownership, stewardship and strategic asset decisions, while the operating brands focus on service delivery and client relationships.

Our Approach

Principles That Guide Us

Clarity of Role

We maintain a clear distinction between asset ownership and day-to-day commercial operations. Clients of LBM Trucks or Palatial Construction engage directly with those brands.

Responsible Stewardship

Assets under our ownership are maintained to high operational standards, properly insured and managed with a view to long-term reliability and value retention.

Measured Growth

We expand the asset portfolio deliberately, aligning new acquisitions with the needs of our operating businesses and broader market opportunities in Zimbabwe.

Professional Integrity

All published information reflects verified assets, services and arrangements. We prioritise accuracy and transparency in our corporate communications.
