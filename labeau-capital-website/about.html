Who We Are

Assets That Support Specialist Operations

Labeau Capital is structured to own and manage productive assets that support the activities of its operating businesses.

Through LBM Trucks and Palatial Construction, the company supports specialised service operations in truck-crane hire, lifting solutions, construction and equipment hire.

By separating asset ownership and management from service delivery, Labeau Capital aims to promote efficient asset utilisation, responsible management and long-term commercial value.

  • Asset ownership and management
  • Specialist service delivery through our brands
  • Long-term commercial value
