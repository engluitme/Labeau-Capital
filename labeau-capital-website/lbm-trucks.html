Truck-mounted crane hire and specialised lifting services for construction, industrial and commercial clients.

Reliable Lifting Capacity When You Need It

LBM Trucks is the operating brand focused on truck-mounted crane hire and related lifting services. We provide mobile lifting solutions for loading, offloading and handling materials or equipment on construction sites, industrial yards and commercial premises.
